# `/who-its-for/foreign-income`

Hero:
**Paid from abroad. Still living and filing here.**

Lead:
The fact that money arrives every month — or even comes with a payslip — does not mean an Indian employer is handling the India side for you.

Use IncomingPaymentRecord.

Sections:
1. How the arrangement differs from Indian payroll.
2. What MFO needs to understand: payer, contract, where work is performed, how money arrives, what documentation exists.
3. What MFO can run: reviewed India-side tax/compliance scope.
4. Foreign-income questions people actually ask.
5. Core vs additional financial support, including FX only as secondary support.
6. Pricing.
7. FAQ.
8. CTA.

Do not publish universal tax treatment statements.
