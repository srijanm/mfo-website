# `/paid-from-abroad`

### Hero

**The money arrives from abroad. The obligations stay here.**

MyFinanceOfficer is a CA firm for people paid by overseas companies and
platforms. We set up and run the India-side tax and compliance, so you know what
needs doing before you need to ask.

`See what I need` · `View pricing →`

### What's actually different here

*Label: Your situation*

**A payslip from abroad is not an Indian payslip.**
It can look exactly like one. It doesn't mean there's an Indian employer
handling anything on your behalf.

**The money can arrive three different ways and leave three different trails.**
A direct transfer, a platform like Deel, a service like Wise. What your bank
records, and what you can prove later, is not the same in each case.

**Your bank may ask about a payment long after it arrived.**
Sometimes years later. What you can produce at that point depends on what was
kept at the time.

**Your contract is with a company that has never dealt with India.**
They're not being difficult. They've genuinely never been asked for the
documents you'll eventually need.

### What we run for you

*Label: Included in the annual fee*

- **Setup and registrations** — what you need now, what you don't need yet, and
  what would change that
- **Income tax** — the ongoing work around the way you actually earn
- **Export and foreign-income compliance** — where relevant to your facts
- **Documentation** — the records that let you prove what you earn to a bank,
  a landlord, a lender or a visa officer
- **Drafts and filing** — you see what's being filed before it's filed
- **Ongoing questions** — one accountable place to get the actual answer

### How we behave

*Label: Before we file anything*

**You see the draft first.** You read it before it goes anywhere. If something
looks wrong, it's still a draft.

**Your contact details stay yours.** Your phone number and email remain on your
own portals.

**You'll know who signs your return.** An ICAI-registered chartered accountant
signs it, and you'll know who before anything is filed.

**The fee is agreed before you start.** Not an estimate, and not a headline
price that grows.

### Price

**Three annual prices. Your scope is agreed before you sign up.**

₹19,999 · ₹24,999 · ₹34,999 per year

Which one fits depends on the work you actually need. We tell you before you
commit.

`See the full pricing →`

### Questions

**I get a payslip from abroad. Is this still for me?**
Probably, yes. The question isn't whether a PDF says "payslip" — it's who is
handling the India side. Tell us how the arrangement works and we'll tell you
what applies to your situation.

**My company says they handle everything.**
They may handle everything on their side. That's a different thing from the
India side, and the India side is usually still yours. Worth checking rather
than assuming.

**I've been doing this for two years already. Is it too late?**
No. It's more common than not. Tell us what's happened so far and we'll tell
you where you stand, including if the honest answer is that you're fine.

**Tell us how you're paid. We'll tell you what you actually need.**

# `/freelancers`

### Hero

**On paper you run a business. In your head you have a job.**

MyFinanceOfficer is a CA firm for freelancers, consultants and independent
professionals. We set up and run the tax and compliance side, so you know what
needs doing before you need to ask.

`See what I need` · `View pricing →`

### What's actually different here

*Label: Your situation*

**Three clients is still a business.**
Most accounting advice assumes volume — inventory, staff, hundreds of
transactions. You have a handful of invoices a month, and almost none of that
advice fits.

**Nobody is doing this in the background for you.**
There's no payroll department quietly handling the parts a salaried friend
never thinks about. Those parts didn't disappear. They just moved to you.

**The questions arrive out of order.**
A client asks for something you've never heard of. You find the answer, and it
raises two more. There's no obvious person to ask, so it waits.

**You are the first person in your family to earn this way.**
Which means the people you'd normally ask have genuinely never had to know.

### What we run for you

*Label: Included in the annual fee*

- **Setup and registrations** — what you need now, what you don't need yet, and
  what would change that
- **Income tax** — the ongoing work around the way you actually earn
- **Invoicing that holds up** — so what you send matches what gets filed
- **Drafts and filing** — you see what's being filed before it's filed
- **Documentation** — records that let you prove what you earn to a bank, a
  landlord, a lender or a visa officer
- **Ongoing questions** — one accountable place to get the actual answer

### How we behave

*Same four rows as `/paid-from-abroad`.*

### Price

*Same block as `/paid-from-abroad`.*

### Questions

**I don't earn enough for this yet.**
That may well be true. Tell us how you earn and where you are today. If the
sensible answer is to come back later, we'll say so.

**My family already has a CA.**

products sent instead of fees — we set up and run the tax and compliance side,

### What's actually different here

*Label: Your situation*

**Income arrives from more places than most accounting expects.**
Platforms, brands directly, agencies, affiliate arrangements, one-off
sponsorships. Each one records the payment differently.

**Some of it isn't a payment at all.**
A product sent to you. A trip covered. An arrangement agreed over DM with no
invoice anywhere. These are real arrangements and they don't stop being real
because nothing was transferred.

**The paperwork is inconsistent because the payers are inconsistent.**
A large brand's finance team and a founder paying you from a personal account
are not going to produce the same records.

**Your income can change shape twice in a year.**
Which makes "what did you do last year" a much less useful question than most
accountants think it is.

# `/how-we-work`

Replaces `/about`. No people, stated plainly rather than avoided.

### Hero

**How we work, and what we won't do.**

A CA firm built for work that became normal before most CA practices were built
for it.

### Why this firm exists

Most CA practices are excellent at what they've done for thirty years. Very
few of them were built around someone invoicing a company abroad, taking brand
deals, or running three client relationships from a laptop.

That isn't a criticism of them. It's a description of a gap. The work changed
faster than the practices did, and the people doing the new work are usually the
first in their family to do it — so there's nobody obvious to ask.

We built a practice for that specific situation, and nothing else.

### We run the year, not just the return

Filing is the visible part and the easy part. The part that goes wrong is the
eleven months before it, where nobody is watching and nothing is due yet.

Every deadline that applies to you sits on our calendar. You hear from us
before you'd have remembered.

### Who signs your return

Every return we file is signed by an ICAI-registered chartered accountant.
You'll know who that is before anything is filed — the name is on your
engagement, not just on the return.

We don't publish the team on this site yet. When that changes, it'll change
here first.

### How we use software

We use software so a small team can do the work of a larger one. It handles
tracking, reminders, drafting and the parts that are genuinely mechanical.

It does not decide what applies to you, and it does not sign anything. A person
is accountable for the professional work, and you know who.

### What we won't do

**We won't sell you a structure you don't need.** If a simple setup is right
for you, we'll say so, even where a more complicated one would bill better for
years.

**We won't keep your credentials.** Your phone number and email stay on your own
portals. You retain access to your own records.

**We won't frighten you into buying.** Some firms in this market run on fear.
We'll state a consequence once, plainly, and leave it there.

**We won't take you on if you don't need us yet.** We'll tell you what would
change that, and you can come back then.
