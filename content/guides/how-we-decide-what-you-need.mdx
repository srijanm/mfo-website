Most people arrive with a version of the same question: what am I supposed to
have done by now? The honest answer is that it depends on facts about you, and
the useful thing we can do is find those facts before deciding anything.

## We start with how the money arrives

Who pays you, what the arrangement says, where the work happens and how the
money reaches you are all things that change what applies. None of them are
guesses; they are things you already know, or things a contract already says.

<Callout label="What this page is">
An explanation of how we work. It contains no guidance about your own
situation, and nothing here decides anything for you.
</Callout>

## Then what already exists

Some people arrive having done nothing. Some arrive having been told to set up
something they may not have needed. Both are ordinary. Working out what is
already in place is faster than assuming.

## Then what changes it

The part people find most useful is not the answer today but knowing what would
make the answer different. That is the thing nobody tells you, and it is the
reason we ask about direction as well as position.

## What we do not do

We do not decide what you need from a form, and nothing on this site applies a
rule to your circumstances. A person reads what you tell us and replies.
