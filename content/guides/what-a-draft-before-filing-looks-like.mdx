Handing work over should not mean losing sight of it. The cycle below is the one
we run, and its only real claim is that nothing is filed while you are still
finding out what it says.

## Prepared

We put the return together from what we have collected through the year. This is
the part that is quicker when the year has been run rather than reconstructed.

## Sent to you

You get the draft. Not a summary of the draft, and not a request to approve
something you have not seen.

<Callout label="What this page is">
A description of a working process. It makes no statement about what any
return should contain.
</Callout>

## Approved

You say yes, or you ask about something first. Questions at this stage are
normal and are the reason the step exists.

## Filed

Only then. Afterwards you have a record of what was filed and who signed it,
which matters later far more than it seems to at the time.
