"We run the calendar" is easy to say and worth being specific about, because the
difference between running something and reminding someone about it is most of
the value.

## The list is ours to hold

Once we understand how you earn, the relevant obligations for your situation go
on to our calendar. You are not asked to keep a copy, and you are not expected
to know which ones apply to you.

## You hear from us first

The point of holding the list is that we start the work before it is urgent. In
practice that means a message from us asking for something, rather than a
message from you asking whether something is due.

<Callout label="What this page is">
A description of how the working relationship runs. It lists no obligations
and no timings, because those depend on facts about you.
</Callout>

## Nothing here is your calendar

This page deliberately names no obligation and no timing. What applies to you
depends on your own facts, and publishing a generic list would invite exactly
the assumption we are trying to remove.

## When it changes

Circumstances move. When something about how you earn changes, the list changes
with it, which is why we ask about direction rather than only about the year in
front of us.
